About Me

    I am currently a tenure-track assistant professor in the School of Intelligence Software and Engineering, Nanjing University. I received my Ph.D. degree from Nanjing University in 2018 under the supervision of Prof. Qing Gu, and received my B.Eng. degree from Central South University in 2011.
    I am recruiting Ph.D., Master and Undergraduate students.
    现招收博士、硕士以及南京大学本科生，对自然语言处理方向感兴趣的同学，欢迎随时邮件联系。

Research Interests

  • Representation Learning and Understanding
    • Text Representation from LLMs
    • Latent Reasoning for LLMs/MLLMs
    • Representation Probing and Analysis
    • Multimodal Representation Alignment
  • Controllable & Trustworthy Generation
    • Inference-Time Steering and Test-Time Adaptation
    • Hallucination Detection and Mitigation
    • Controllable Image/Video Generation
    • Concept Erasure and Model Watermarking
